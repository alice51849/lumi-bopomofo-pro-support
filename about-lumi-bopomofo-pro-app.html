Lumi Bopomofo Pro: product fact card

1. What it is

Lumi Bopomofo Pro: Zhuyin is the complete edition of the Lumi Bopomofo kids' app for iPhone and iPad (also on Google Play): all 37 Zhuyin sounds, blending, tones and printable worksheets in one paid download.

  • Sound Train: link initial, medial and final into a syllable.
  • Tone Coaster: hear and tell the five tones apart.
  • Listen-and-match, stroke-order tracing, Bopomofo Fishing and the Star Race.
  • Today's Review brings back sounds a child has not mastered.
  • Parent Zone: progress view and printable tracing-and-quiz worksheets of learned or recently missed sounds.

2. Who it is for

  • Preschoolers just starting Bopomofo.
  • Kids getting ready for first grade.
  • Chinese heritage families building a strong pronunciation base.

3. How pricing works

Lumi Bopomofo Pro is a paid download. One purchase includes everything: all 37 sounds, every level and every game. There are no in-app purchases, no subscription, no ads and no sign-up. It works without an internet connection and stores progress only on the device. The current price is shown on the App Store page.

Questions and answers

What is Lumi Bopomofo Pro?

The complete paid edition of Lumi Bopomofo for learning and practicing all 37 Zhuyin sounds, blending, tones and stroke order.

Are there in-app purchases?

No. One purchase includes everything.

Is there a subscription?

No.

Does it need internet, an account, or show ads?

No. The listing states no ads, no sign-up and no internet required, and progress stays on the device.

Can I print worksheets?

Yes. The Parent Zone can print tracing-and-quiz worksheets of learned or recently missed sounds.

How is it different from the free Lumi Bopomofo?

The free app lets a child play the first symbol, then unlocks everything with a one-time in-app purchase. Pro is a paid download that already includes everything, plus the printable worksheets described in its listing. See free vs Pro.
