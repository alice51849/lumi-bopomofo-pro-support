A Bopomofo app for kids starting Chinese school: 4 questions before you buy

One purchase, no ads, no in-app purchases, five games. If your child is about to start a weekend Chinese school or a class that teaches Bopomofo (Zhuyin), these are the questions parents settle before buying Lumi Bopomofo Pro. Everything below is from its App Store listing.

4 questions for parents of a child starting Chinese school

Will it match what my child learns in class?

It covers the core of Bopomofo: all 37 sounds, blending initials, medials and finals into a complete sound, and telling the five tones apart. One purchase unlocks every level and every game, with no ads, no in-app purchase pop-ups and no sign-up.

My child is just starting Bopomofo. What will they practise?

Kids recognise and read all 37 symbols, trace each one in stroke order with Magic Tracing, and play Match and Feed, Bopomofo Fishing and the Star Race. Today's Review brings back the sounds your child hasn't mastered, so practice follows the sounds your child hasn't mastered yet.

Does it go beyond single symbols to blending and tones?

Yes. On the Sound Train kids link initial, medial and final into a complete sound, and the Tone Roller Coaster helps them tell all five tones apart. The listing describes it as blending and tones practice for kids getting ready for first grade.

Can we practise away from the screen, and is it private?

From the Parent Zone you can print tracing-and-quiz worksheets of learned or recently missed sounds for screen-free practice. The app collects no personal data, and all progress stays on your child's device. It runs on iPhone and iPad, and there is an Android version on Google Play.

Starting Bopomofo this term? Lumi Bopomofo Pro is one purchase with everything included: all 37 sounds, five games, blending and tones, printable worksheets. No ads, no in-app purchases, no sign-up.
