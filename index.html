What Lumi Bopomofo Pro: Zhuyin does

Help your child fall in love with Chinese phonics through one adorable space adventure.

Lumi Bopomofo Pro is the complete edition of our Zhuyin and Bopomofo learning game, made for children ages 3 to 7. It turns the 37 Bopomofo sounds into a playful cosmic journey, so kids learn pronunciation, blending and tones naturally as they play. This helps build a strong foundation for preschool and first grade Mandarin.

Features at a glance

  • One purchase, everything unlocked. All 37 sounds, every level and every game.
  • Zero distractions. No ads, no in app purchase pop ups, no sign up, no internet required.
  • Private and safe. The app collects no personal data. All progress stays on your child device.
  • Recognize and read all 37 Bopomofo symbols, from the first sound to full blending.
  • Blending. Combine initials, medials and finals into a complete sound.
  • Tones. Learn to tell the five tones apart through interactive practice.
  • Stroke order and symbol shapes. Trace with a finger to write neatly.
  • Match and Feed. Feed cute companions the right sound.
  • Magic Tracing. Follow the strokes and master the shapes.
  • Tone Roller Coaster. Hear and learn the tones in motion.

Privacy at a glance

The short version: no account needed, no personal data collected, no third-party advertising, no third-party analytics.
