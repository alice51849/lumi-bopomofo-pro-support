Learning Zhuyin alongside your child: 4 questions from parents

Plenty of parents want their child to learn Bopomofo even though they never learned it themselves. Here is a short explanation of what Bopomofo is, and how Lumi Bopomofo Pro lets a child practise without a parent who reads it. App details come from the App Store listing.

4 questions from parents

What is Bopomofo, in one minute?

Bopomofo, also called Zhuyin, is the set of phonetic symbols used in Taiwan to spell out Mandarin sounds. The name comes from its first symbols, ㄅ ㄆ ㄇ ㄈ. Children learn the symbols, then combine them into syllables and add a tone mark, which lets them read words they can already say.

I can't read Bopomofo. Can my child still learn it from an app?

Yes. Lumi Bopomofo Pro uses big buttons and clear voice prompts so young children can play on their own, and every sound is practised by listening and matching. Today's Review brings back the sounds your child hasn't mastered, and a parents page shows learning progress, so you can follow along without reading the symbols yourself.

How does my child learn to write the symbols?

Magic Tracing has kids follow each stroke with a finger to learn stroke order and symbol shapes. From the Parent Zone you can also print tracing-and-quiz worksheets of sounds your child has learned or recently missed, for practice away from the screen.

Which devices does it work on?

It is a universal app for iPhone and iPad, and no internet is required. There is also an Android version on Google Play.
