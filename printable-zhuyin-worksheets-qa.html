What is on the printable Zhuyin worksheets?

Tracing and quiz practice. From the Parent Zone in Lumi Bopomofo Pro you print tracing-and-quiz worksheets, so your child can practise stroke order and symbol shapes on paper and then check what they remember.

Which sounds go on the worksheet?

The worksheets cover sounds your child has already learned or recently missed. That keeps the paper practice on the symbols that need it instead of a generic page of all 37.

Use them as the screen-free half of the same routine. In the app, Magic Tracing has the child follow the strokes with a finger and Today's Review brings back the sounds they haven't mastered; the worksheet then takes those same sounds to paper. The parents page shows learning progress anytime.

No. The listing says one purchase includes everything, with no ads and no in-app purchases: all 37 sounds, every level and every game. The app works on iPhone and iPad and can be shared with the whole family.
