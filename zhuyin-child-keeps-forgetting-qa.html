4 questions parents ask

Why does my child forget Zhuyin symbols they knew last week?

Many of the 37 symbols look or sound alike (ㄅ and ㄆ, ㄉ and ㄊ, ㄓ and ㄗ), and a symbol seen once a week has little chance to stick. What helps is short, frequent practice that comes back to the exact symbols your child is still missing, instead of starting from ㄅ every time.

How does Lumi Bopomofo Pro bring back the sounds my child misses?

Today's Review brings back the sounds your child hasn't mastered, and weak spot review keeps those symbols in rotation. The sounds come back inside the games: listen-and-match in Match and Feed, stroke-order tracing in Magic Tracing, Bopomofo Fishing and the Star Race. Daily goals and achievement badges give a reason to come back each day.

Can we review away from the screen?

Yes. From the Parent Zone you can print tracing-and-quiz worksheets of learned or recently missed sounds. A parents page also shows learning progress, so you can see which symbols to go over together.
