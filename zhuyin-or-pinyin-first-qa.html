4 questions parents ask

Should my child learn Zhuyin or Pinyin first?

Start with the system your child's class or family uses. Zhuyin (Bopomofo) is what first graders in Taiwan learn, and many weekend Chinese schools with Taiwanese roots teach it; schools following a mainland China curriculum teach Pinyin. If your child's class uses Zhuyin, Lumi Bopomofo Pro is one purchase with no ads and five games built around all 37 Zhuyin symbols.

Will starting with Zhuyin make Pinyin harder later?

Both systems write the same Mandarin sounds and tones, so the listening work carries over: hearing an initial, a final and a tone is the same skill in either script. What changes is the symbols. In Lumi Bopomofo Pro kids practise exactly that listening work: on the Sound Train they link initial, medial and final into a complete sound, and the Tone Roller Coaster helps them tell all five tones apart.

Does Lumi Bopomofo Pro teach Pinyin too?

No. It is a Zhuyin app: all 37 Bopomofo symbols, blending, tones and stroke-order tracing. One purchase unlocks every level and all five games (Match and Feed, Magic Tracing, Tone Roller Coaster, Bopomofo Fishing and the Star Race), with no ads, no in-app purchases and no sign-up. If your child's class teaches Pinyin, it is not the right fit.

How do we keep Zhuyin practice going at home?

Today's Review brings back the sounds your child hasn't mastered, and from the Parent Zone you can print tracing-and-quiz worksheets of learned or recently missed sounds for practice away from the screen. No internet is required, the app collects no personal data, and progress stays on your child's device. It runs on iPhone and iPad, and there is an Android version on Google Play.
